# VIK José Ignacio · Room photography

Three slots per room. Drop a replacement over any filename below and
the site picks it up — no code change and no renaming.

* `-01` the room itself. Used in the carousel and behind the room name.
* `-02` `-03` second and third views. Used as the thumbnails.

**STAND-IN** means that slot currently holds a photograph of the house
rather than of the room, because the source site publishes only one
image for it. Those are the ones worth shooting first.
