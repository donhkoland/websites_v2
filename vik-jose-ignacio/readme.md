# VIK José Ignacio

One destination. Three ways to experience it.

**The first approved design direction for the VIK web platform.**

**[Explore the live preview ↗](https://donhkoland.github.io/websites_v2/vik-jose-ignacio/)** · [Visit the Styleguide ↗](https://donhkoland.github.io/websites_v2/styleguide/)

## The feel

An editorial approach to the coast: warm paper, generous space, expressive typography and film that lets the place speak. Estancia, Playa and Bahía share one story, with room for each retreat's character.

## The experience

- A film-led arrival with booking close at hand.
- Three retreats, a seasonal guide and a shared world of art, dining and experiences.
- Live weather, a destination map, press and a clear path to a stay.
- Responsive imagery, mobile film variants and reduced-motion support.

## Built to share

HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with the page's imagery, films and typefaces kept together. Weather and map tiles use online services.

The [Styleguide](../styleguide/) brings the platform's reusable modules into one dedicated space.
