About

A haven of art, nature and high design.

White adobe,
red tin, open country

Estancia VIK José Ignacio was a collaboration between the creators, Carrie and Alex Vik, and the Uruguayan architect Marcelo Daglio — a traditional Spanish-style estancia of white adobe walls and red tin roofs, with covered walkways linking courtyards and gardens.

Within the 50,000 square feet of the structure, more than half is outdoor space: interior patios, courtyards, covered walkways and a large organic herb garden. The patios carry outdoor seating, sculpture installations and shade from the afternoon.

The south side opens onto a grass garden and a twenty-metre pool cut from Absolute Nero stone. The floor of the pool is dotted with fibre-optic lights that mirror the constellations above it.

The house

Galpón, chapel, cellar, gardens.

Location

Four thousand acres, a mile from the Atlantic.

The Estancia sits in the rolling countryside of José Ignacio, bordering the Arroyo and the Laguna José Ignacio, which meet the Southern Atlantic just a mile away as the crow flies.
