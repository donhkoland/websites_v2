One address,
not a logistics plan

Most destination weddings are three suppliers and a coach schedule. Here the venue, the beds, the kitchens and the wine belong to the same house, so the whole thing is arranged by one team and answered in one thread.

Pavilion VIK holds 450 seated or 1,000 standing, opens straight onto the beach, and sits minutes from Bahía, Playa and Estancia. The ceremony can be on the sand, the dinner under the skylights and the last hour back outside, without anyone leaving the property.

Smaller occasions do not need the dome at all. A table for twenty in the walled garden, a bonfire at La Susana, or the whole of Playa VIK taken for a family — the same team, a shorter conversation.
