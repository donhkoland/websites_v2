System

Thirty-two blocks, and the reason for each.

Every module below is the live one. The first sixteen are cut straight out of the page where they work, so a block that breaks in the system breaks here on the same afternoon — instead of being remembered as it looked the day someone took a picture of it.

The last sixteen are the library the first system built and this one has not needed yet: overlays, rooms, enquiry, specification. They are here in the new language, on a stylesheet of their own, so nothing already approved can be disturbed by them. Which ones stay is a later conversation.

Nothing on this page carries a rule of its own. A module that needed styling to look right in a catalogue would not be a module. The reason each one exists sits in the HTML beside it, not on the screen.

M10 Full-bleed band with calendar .film + .season[data-season-table]
When to come

Know where to stay.
Then know when to come.

José Ignacio transforms across the year. Peak season is vibrant, social and full of events. March and the shoulder season are quieter, more intimate and restorative. The countryside is at its best when the coast empties.

Dec – JanPeakVibrant, energetic, social. Events, dining and nightlife every evening.
FebruaryFull summerLong days on the water. The beach clubs at their best.
MarchShoulderQuieter, more intimate, restorative. Space and disconnection.
Apr – NovCountrysideEstancia comes into its own. Horses, fire and empty landscape.

    L06 The accordion .accordion + .acc-item
    01What time can we check in?

    From three in the afternoon, and we hold the room until you arrive however late that turns out to be. If you land in the morning, tell your Journey Designer and we open it early whenever the house allows.

    02Can we move between the three houses?

    Yes, and a good number of guests do. Dining, wellness and every experience are open to anyone staying at any of the three, and the transfers between them are arranged for you.

    03Is the countryside far from the beach?

    Forty minutes by road, and the two feel like different countries. That distance is the point rather than the inconvenience — it is why one week can hold both.
