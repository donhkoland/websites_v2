Stay · Three retreats

Choose the retreat, and the trip chooses itself.

Staying at VIK José Ignacio is not about choosing a room. It is about choosing a very specific kind of experience, shaped by the property you stay in and the season in which you visit.

M06Intro editorialsource · wellness .intro · destination + philosophy copy

One connected
destination

VIK José Ignacio is not three separate hotels. It is one ecosystem, held together by dining, wellness, art, horses, nature, events and the Journey Designer team.

Each retreat has its own landscape, architecture and reason to choose it. Playa VIK is contemporary, intimate and ocean-driven. Estancia VIK is rooted in nature, horses and open countryside. Bahía VIK is relaxed, design-led and social, right on the bay.

José Ignacio itself changes through the year. Late December and January are vibrant and social. March and the shoulder season are quieter, more intimate and restorative. The website tells you not only where to stay, but when to come.

The VIK pillars

Created through art. Rooted in nature and culture. Yours to experience.

01 · Art & Design

Creativity, made physical

Art, architecture, interiors, furniture and creative collaboration. Every VIK building is a commission, not a template.

02 · Nature & Culture

Shaped by place

Unique destinations, traditions and heritage. Each retreat is formed by its landscape, local identity and seasonal rhythm.

03 · Wine

Terroir as a language

Vineyard, winemaking, viticulture, architecture and vineyard experiences — a global VIK asset from Millahue to José Ignacio.

04 · Dining

Where flavour meets nourishment

Local, seasonal, farm-to-table and open fire. Dining as a shared cultural experience rather than a service.

05 · Wellbeing

How guests feel

Movement, recovery, restoration, nourishment, nature, sleep, social connection and balance.

06 · Hospitality

Intuitive and personal

Journey Designers, personal itineraries, recognition, flexibility and human service. Warm, but sophisticated.

Culinary philosophy

What guides every plate.

Hover a line. Rows with a photograph reveal it; rows without one simply stay quiet.

01

Local and seasonal sourcing

Producers within reach of the property. The menu follows the season, not the other way round.

02

Farm to table

Estancia VIK grows and rears much of what reaches its own tables, from garden beds to the parrilla.

03

Open-fire cooking

The Uruguayan asador, treated as a discipline. Slow heat, whole cuts, embers and time.

04

Coastal and countryside cuisine

Fish from the Atlantic at CieloMar and Zodiaco. Fire and field at El Asador.

05

Healthy and pleasurable

Fresh, organic and light — without ever becoming an exercise in restraint. This row has no image on purpose.

06

VIK Wines at the centre

Millahue Valley on every list. Dining as a shared cultural experience.

    Contact & FAQ

    Before you arrive.

    Reservations are confirmed through Bookassist, with PayPal accepted for the José Ignacio properties. Cancellation terms vary by season and by property — peak season carries a longer notice period.
    Check-in from 15:00, check-out by 12:00. Early arrival and late departure can usually be arranged — ask your Journey Designer before travelling.
    Estancia VIK and Bahía VIK are the most family-oriented retreats. Share children's ages before arrival so rooms, activities and dining can be arranged around them.
    Pets are considered case by case. Contact the team before booking.
    Accessible accommodation is available at selected properties. Tell us what you need and we will match you to the right retreat and room.
