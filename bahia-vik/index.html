About

Adobe, corten,
zinc and glass

Bahía VIK José Ignacio is built around a collection of uniquely designed bungalows, several of them in privileged beachfront settings, and ten suites on the first and second floors of the main building.

Each bungalow has a distinct exterior, an art-filled interior and its own design scheme — adobe, corten steel, zinc, glass, thatch, timber and stone. Tucked into the beach dunes, each one deliberately blends interior and exterior to encourage the maximum enjoyment of nature.

Natural dunes covered in swaying beach grasses and local flora divide one bungalow from the next, so each keeps its own privacy while sharing the same stretch of the bay.

Location

In the dunes, on the calm side.

Bahía VIK sits on Ruta 10 in the dunes of Playa Mansa — the sheltered side of José Ignacio, where the water stays calm and the beach is walkable from every bungalow.

    Accommodations

    Ten suites, fifteen bungalows.

    Ten suites in the main building, each one given to an artist before it was given to a guest. Fifteen bungalows in the dunes around it, no two built from the same material.

    Suites · Ten, in the main building Bungalows · Fifteen, in the dunes
    Suites

    Ten suites on two floors of the main building, above the bay. Each was handed to a Uruguayan artist who worked in the room before anyone slept in it — the headboard, the tiles and the light are the work, not a decoration hung on it afterwards.

    Bungalows

    Fifteen bungalows set into the dunes around the house, each built from a different material — adobe, corten, zinc, glass, thatch, timber. Beach grass grows between them, so each keeps its own privacy while sharing the same stretch of Playa Mansa.
