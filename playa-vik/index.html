About

A collaboration with Carlos Ott.

Titanium, bronze
and the ocean

Playa VIK is a collaboration between the creators, Carrie and Alex Vik, and the internationally renowned Uruguayan architect Carlos Ott. The avant-garde beachfront retreat is built around a central Sculpture building, which holds an important collection of international and contemporary Uruguayan art.

The building's 55-foot inclined façade opens through a vast sliding wall of glass onto the coastline. The entrance is a two-storey curved and inclined glass façade with a monumental sculpted bronze door by Pablo Atchugarry — a modern reading of Ghiberti's doors at the Baptistery in Florence.

The lateral walls and roof are double-curved titanium panels. Patagonian ebony floors run throughout, extending from the living room to the pool terrace and blurring the line between interior and exterior.

Location

On the point, above Mansa beach.

Playa VIK sits on the Mansa beach in the bohemian village of José Ignacio, twelve miles north-east of Punta del Este, with a panoramic view of the landscape and the ocean beyond.

    The Sculpture, and six casas.

    Suites within the Sculpture building, flanked by six casas of two or three bedrooms each with private gardens. Every casa has a fireplace and a distinct art-filled interior; retractable windows open the rooms onto the gardens, under living roofs of rayitos del sol wildflowers.
