Nothing here is
an excursion

There is no activities desk, no laminated menu and no coach waiting outside. What there is instead is a person who knows the coast, the countryside and the weather, and who books the whole thing in a message.

Most of what follows happens on VIK land or within twenty minutes of it: four thousand acres of countryside, twelve kilometres of coast, a lagoon, and an art collection that was commissioned room by room rather than bought at auction.

A day here has
four different weathers

The wind decides more than the calendar does. Mornings are still and belong to the land; the breeze arrives at midday and the water becomes the better idea; the light goes long after six; and whatever happened, the day ends at a fire. Before you book one

The questions that come up.

Most things, the day before. Riding, the lagoon and the curator's walk are better with two or three days, and in January they are better still with a week. Ask anyway — cancellations happen constantly.
No. The morning ride is at a walk across flat country and takes complete beginners, including children from about eight. The sunrise ride moves faster and is for people who have ridden recently, not people who rode once in 2009.
The water moves to the other beach or to the following day, and nothing is charged for a cancellation the wind caused. The land, the collection and the kitchens are unaffected — the countryside is arguably better in rain.
Everything on this page is open to guests of Estancia VIK, Playa VIK and Bahía VIK, whichever one you booked, and transfers between the three are arranged for you. Non-resident access is case by case — write and ask.
Riding, the garden, paddling on Mansa, tennis and the game rooms all work with children. Tell us their ages when you write and the day gets built around them rather than around us.
Some of it is included in the stay — movement classes, the game rooms, the beaches, the collection. Guided riding, the lagoon, fishing, treatments and the cellar are charged separately and quoted before you commit, never after.
