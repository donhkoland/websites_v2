The room is
the commission

At most hotels a room type is a size and a view. Here it is a person: the artist who made the work, the material the house is built from, and the exact place it stands on the site.

Estancia VIK has four master suites and eight guest suites, each signed by a different Uruguayan artist. Playa VIK has six casas scattered on the point and six suites under the titanium roof. Bahía VIK has eleven bungalows in the dunes — adobe, corten, zinc, glass, thatch, timber and stone, no two the same — and five suites in the main building.
