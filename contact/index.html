    Before you arrive

    The questions that come up.

    Reservations are confirmed through Bookassist, with PayPal accepted for the José Ignacio properties. Cancellation terms vary by season and by property — peak season carries a longer notice period. The direct rate is the best available, and the Journey Designer is included in it.
    Check-in from 15:00, check-out by 12:00. Early arrival and late departure can usually be arranged — ask before travelling rather than on the day, because in January the room genuinely may not be free.
    Bahía for families and company, Playa for couples and privacy, Estancia for horses, space and the whole of the rest of the year. If that has not settled it, the chooser lays the three cases side by side and a Journey Designer will simply tell you.
    Yes. Dining, wellness, the horses, the collection and the venue are open to guests of any of the three, and the transfers between them are arranged for you. Where you sleep changes the holiday; it does not fence it in.
    Estancia VIK and Bahía VIK are the most family-oriented. Share children's ages before arrival so the rooms, the riding and the dining are arranged around them rather than adjusted on the day.
    December and January are full and social. February is the long-day summer. March is warm, empty and the month people who live here prefer. From April the coast quietens and the countryside becomes the reason to travel.
    Considered case by case. Ask before booking rather than after.
    Accessible accommodation is available at selected properties, and the terrain differs a great deal between the dunes, the point and the countryside. Tell us what you need and we will match you to the right house and the right room, not the nearest one.
